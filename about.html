About

I’m Shant Vartanian, a 3D artist in Barcelona making architectural visualization: stills and films that tell the story of a design through light, material and camera.

I studied Architectural Technology at George Brown College in Toronto, then spent three years at Urbis Engineering drafting residential and mid-rise projects in Revit and modeling them in Rhino.

At Design Center in Yerevan I was the main 3D artist on interior projects, including restaurants and bars: modeling in Rhino, showing quick options to clients in D5, and producing final visuals and VR walkthroughs in Unreal Engine. Since 2025 I’ve worked freelance with international clients.
