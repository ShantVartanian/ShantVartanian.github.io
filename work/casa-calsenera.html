Casa Calsenera

Private house · Film and stills · 2026

Watch on Vimeo

A foggy autumn morning. As the mist lifts, a low sun breaks through the trees and opens a way into the house, across dark brick, white walls and oak.

Architecture and interior design by Vincent Van Duysen, recreated as a personal study. Everything in the scene was built by me: the architecture, furniture and props were modeled in Blender, and every tree and plant was created in SpeedTree.

Music: ‘Reverie Tide’ by Abstract Aprils on Uppbeat. License code 6HUHW6SK0CZ5JMBW.
