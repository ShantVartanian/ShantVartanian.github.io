Sand & Concrete — Santa Monica

Coastal villa · Film and stills · 2026

Watch on Vimeo

Early morning on the West Coast, at the start of a hot summer day. Low sun rakes across raw concrete and sandy ground, while the ocean sits soft and pale beyond the trees.

Every part of the villa, from the architecture to the furniture and props, was modeled in Blender. The terrain was built from real satellite data and refined in Gaea, then dressed with Quixel Megascans vegetation and rocks. The camera stays steady and composed, with a few handheld shots for a more lived-in feel.

Music: ‘Wishes And Sighs’ by Abstract Aprils on Uppbeat. License code MLXATUDKQHNYGELQ.
